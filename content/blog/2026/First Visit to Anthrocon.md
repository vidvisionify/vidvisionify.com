* Staying at *Wyndam Grand*; 
	* In a double room, There's only enough floor space for a twin-sized air mattress, if you happen to have an extra person in your party.
	* The Samsung TVs in the rooms don't have buttons, and it's a weird hotel TV remote. If you want to use HDMI, you'll need to bring a universal remote or bring a Flipper Zero
	* Advantage of this hotel, you are closer to market square, where most of the food is.
	* Parking is ass, and because of this they temporarily moved the shuttle

* 'Local' Food
	* **Primanti Bros** - Had a *New Yorker,* didn't expect the coleslaw (usually not a fan), but tasted great! Also had a *Drunk Duck* which is much larger than the photo in the menu made it seem.  *3/5* ⭐⭐⭐
	* **Pizza Milano** - Recommended by the hotel, if you like *Little Caesar's*, it's pretty similar. I've been told I should have tried *Pizza Bari* instead.
	* **Bodega Alcoholic 'Slushies'** -  *4/5* ⭐⭐⭐⭐
	* **Westin**  -  I forgot the name of this one... The food in the lobby of the Westin. I only had a corndog, and it hit when I really needed food. This was very busy most of the times I passed it.
	* **City Works** - We did a 'family dinner' with friends of ours from another hotel room here. One of the better meals we all had in Pittsburgh. Take advantage of an offer they have in their menu to get a free appetizer when you sign up for their newsletter! They have handheld registers, so separate checks didn't take too long. *4/5* ⭐⭐⭐⭐
	* **Condado Taco** - I made a mistake by getting a burrito bowl, I just didn't think the tacos were going to be big enough. Bud Box (maybe +1 taco) is plenty of food. We had it delivered, but supposedly inside seating is neat? *3/5* ⭐⭐⭐
