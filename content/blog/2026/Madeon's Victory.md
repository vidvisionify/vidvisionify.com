March 2025, I was excited to see a new era of Madeon's music was approaching with an live show debut. He's done shows in Red Rocks before, but the last one was delayed a few days by snow. Presale tickets were some of the cheapest I've seen through ticketmaster. I decided the flight was worth it to see one of my favorite musical artists, see a new city, and cross the Mississippi river for the first time.
