A collection of tips I learned from [[Past Events|past years]] of going to Midwest Furfest, although most of these apply to most conventions.
* Ideally get the main hotel, I suppose the shuttle will get you by to a point
* Just walk around the con center, avoid skywalk if the weather isn't terrible
* This isn't actually Chicago, it's safer than your anxiety thinks.
* Avoid getting sick: basic stuff, wash your hands before a meal
