#### 🍊 About

###### 💭 Inspiration

- Hope and building tomorrow
- Based on the [Gurem by Nukude](https://nukude.gumroad.com/l/gurem), the first model I fell in love with in #VRChat

###### 📖 Lore

- A twist on _Protogen_ lore, occasionally the clones are deemed not fit for combat and placed in other roles, to be given armor/upgrades later. Never really picking up any proper skills, he is thrown to a scrapping planet to sort for valuable minerals (there are none, and he is the only one in the outpost) from a past civilization. During an inspection, he steals a spacecraft and adventures ensue.
- Interested by earth trinkets and media, collects when he can.
- Has basic electronics skills.
- His clothes are actually high visibility for electricians, but was the only comfortable set he could find.
- Goggles do act as screens for a micro-computer, networking disabled but has access to an encyclopedia of knowledge. Syncs with brainwaves to control.
- I haven't entirely decided if [[Slate]] and Vid are in the same universe.
